Billet Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Developer Eric Janusson

Notes and preferences

The Mac App Store edition of Billet stores your notes, archive, recovered copies and prompt templates as plain-text files in its macOS app container. Billet has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party crash-reporting integration. Billet does not send your notes or model outputs to the developer. Your macOS backups may include Billet’s files.

If you use Storage → Import Old Notes…, Billet reads the folder you select and copies notes into its own storage. It leaves the source folder unchanged. The Mac App Store edition offers no sync. Some personal builds can read from and write to a sync folder you choose, and a separately provisioned developer build can use Apple’s iCloud Documents. Your storage provider may sync files under its own terms. Disconnect a chosen sync folder in the storage menu to stop future sync. Copies already in the folder remain there.

Apple Intelligence, Shortcuts and the clipboard

Compact and Agentify use Apple’s on-device model through the Foundation Models framework. Billet does not send the text to an AI server. If a result is unavailable, Compact shows the original text and leaves it in its slot. Agentify leaves the note and clipboard as they are when it cannot produce a prompt.

The Add Thought action stores the text passed to it by Shortcuts. Apple services and any apps to which you paste or send text handle that text under their own policies. For Apple services, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Billet writes to the clipboard when you use a copy action. It may check whether the clipboard has changed, without reading its contents. Billet registers a single global shortcut to open its panel.

Retention and deletion

Notes are kept until you edit or delete them. To clear a slot, delete its text in Billet. To review or remove stored files, reveal the app’s files with Storage → Open Notes Folder. Quit Billet before removing files directly. Separately remove any copies you kept in backups, a sync folder or other apps to the extent those services allow.

Support and privacy questions

If you email eric@ericjanusson.ca, Eric receives your address and anything you choose to include. He uses the correspondence to answer and follow up, keeps it as needed for that purpose, and accepts requests to delete it at the same address. Please omit sensitive note text from support requests.

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
