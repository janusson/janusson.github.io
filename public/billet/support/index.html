Billet Support

For help with the Mac App Store edition, email eric@ericjanusson.ca. Include your macOS version, the Billet version and build shown under Storage → About Billet, and the steps that led to the issue. Use sample text in screenshots or bug reports.

Open Billet and start writing

Billet lives in the menu bar. Click the note icon or press ⌃⌥Space (Control–Option–Space). If another app uses that shortcut, open Billet from its icon. If your menu bar is crowded, make room for the icon or reopen Billet from Applications.

Use ⌘1–⌘5 to switch slots, ⌘N to find an empty slot and Esc to close the panel. Archive saves your original note as a dated Markdown file and frees up its slot.

Find, back up or import notes

Use Storage → Open Notes Folder to find your notes. To back them up, quit Billet and copy that folder. Use Storage → Import Old Notes… to copy notes from a backup or an older Billet folder. The Mac App Store edition stores notes on the Mac where you use it. Conflicting copies may appear in Recovered.

Apple Intelligence

Compact and Agentify require a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with the local model enabled and ready. You can still write, switch slots, archive and copy notes while the model is unavailable.
