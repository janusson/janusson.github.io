# Contact

I’m based in British Columbia, Canada.

<h2 id="hiring">Hiring</h2>

I’m interested in senior roles in analytical development, mass spectrometry, CMC, laboratory
technical leadership, and scientific software. Share the role, location or work arrangement, and
what the team needs this hire to solve. See my [professional profile](/about/) and
[laboratory outcomes](/#measurement).

<h2 id="consulting">Consulting &amp; collaborations</h2>

I take on scoped projects in LC-MS/MS data review, analytical method troubleshooting, and
scientific Python workflows. A project might involve reviewing one LC-MS/MS batch and documenting QC
findings, or assessing a data-processing step and proposing a reproducible improvement.

For an initial discussion, tell me what you’ve measured, what decision you need to make, and your
timeline. An email summary is enough to get started.
