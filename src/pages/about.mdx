I'm an analytical chemist and mass spectrometrist based in British Columbia. I develop and validate
analytical methods, commission laboratory instruments, and write Python software to analyse the data
they produce.

## Laboratory work

- **Analytical development and laboratory operations | TMC Manufacturing (2024 – 2026)**

  I developed and validated trace-element methods for stable-isotope production. I helped bring
  testing in-house, wrote the capital proposal for ion chromatography and ICP-MS, and worked with
  the vendor on ICP-MS commissioning. My work also included GMP batch review, deviation
  investigations, and stability testing.

- **Lead Chemist | Delic Labs (2021 – 2022)**

  I helped commission the lab, validated methods, and wrote quality documentation aligned with
  ISO/IEC 17025. I used untargeted LC-MS/MS to profile complex samples and developed a Python
  workflow for processing and annotating the spectra.

## Research

- **Postdoctoral Research Associate | University of Glasgow (2017 – 2020)**

  I used travelling-wave ion mobility and high-resolution mass spectrometry to study how
  polyoxometalate clusters assemble. In one study, we compared fructose and glucose as reducing
  agents. Fructose favoured one Wells–Dawson isomer under the conditions we tested. I wrote Python
  scripts to process the mass spectrometry data.
  [Read the cluster chemistry paper](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/186345/).

- **PhD Researcher | University of Victoria (2012 – 2017)**

  I studied how solvent composition and the position of the electrospray emitter affect ion
  signals. For my dissertation, I developed methods to monitor reactions by mass spectrometry in
  real time. In one study, I used pressurized sample infusion to follow charged species as
  phosphine ligands activated a palladium precatalyst. We used UV–Vis to track the neutral
  precatalyst.
  [Read the palladium paper](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2017/cc/c6cc08824d).

developing MassPilot, a private tool for reviewing the evidence behind LC-HRMS annotations.
