# About Me

I am an independent analytical chemist, mass spectrometrist, and scientific software developer. My work sits at the intersection of experimental analytical chemistry, high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS), and automated scientific computing.

## Focus & Expertise

- **Mass Spectrometry & Separations**: Method development and data interpretation across LC-MS/MS, GC-MS, TWIMS, Orbitrap, QTOF, and ICP-MS platforms.
- **Spectral Similarity & Networking**: Development of similarity scoring algorithms, automated spectral library generation, and molecular networking for MS/MS fragmentation data.
- **Data Pipelines & Automation**: Config-driven, version-controlled workflows for high-throughput spectral annotation, feature extraction, and laboratory data management.
- **AI × Mass Spectrometry**: Building Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to enable direct, programmatically queryable access to mass spectrometry datasets for AI tools and workflows.
- **Regulated Laboratories**: Design and implementation of analytical methods and software pipelines compliant with ISO/IEC 17025 standards.

## Open-Source & Research

I build open-source tools to make mass spectrometry data processing more reproducible, transparent, and accessible. Projects like **MassFlow** and **MSMCP** focus on replacing vendor-locked GUI operations with reproducible, code-first workflows.
