# About Me

I'm an analytical chemist and scientific software developer working at the intersection of mass spectrometry, laboratory instrumentation, and reproducible data analysis. I earned a PhD in analytical chemistry at the University of Victoria and carried out postdoctoral research at the University of Glasgow.

My research and industry work spans LC-MS/MS, GC-MS, ion mobility mass spectrometry, analytical method development, instrument troubleshooting, and laboratory quality systems. I've built analytical workflows and documentation for research and regulated laboratory settings, and I use Python to make scientific data easier to inspect and reproduce.

My open-source work includes [MassFlow](https://github.com/janusson/MassFlow), for MS/MS data processing and candidate retrieval, and [MSMCP](https://github.com/janusson/MSMCP), which makes mass spectrometry data accessible to software agents. I care about scientific traceability, honest uncertainty, and tools that support—not replace—expert judgment.
