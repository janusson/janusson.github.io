# Billet Privacy Policy

**Effective:** November 24, 2026

Billet is a menu-bar app for macOS made by Eric Janusson, an independent developer in Canada. This
policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Billet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Your notes stay on
your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app has no
permission to connect to the internet.

## Your notes

Billet saves your notes as plain Markdown text files inside the app's own storage on your Mac (its
sandboxed Application Support folder). This includes the five slots, the archive, your prompt
templates, and any preserved copies Billet makes when two versions of a file conflict. You can open
this folder at any time from the **Storage** menu and read, copy, back up, or delete the files with
any app you like.

The developer never receives your notes. Deleting Billet and its folder removes them from your Mac.

The Mac App Store edition of Billet does not sync. **Import Old Notes…** reads a folder you choose,
copies notes in, and never changes that folder. Editions distributed outside the Mac App Store can
copy notes into a sync folder you choose; any cloud service holding that folder moves the files under
its own terms, and Billet uploads nothing itself.

## AI features (Compact and Agentify)

Compact and Agentify use Apple's on-device Foundation Models, part of Apple Intelligence. The text you
transform is processed on your Mac. Billet does not send it to the developer, to Apple, or to any
other AI service. These features only run when you ask them to.

## Clipboard

Billet writes to your clipboard only when you choose an action that copies, such as **Copy Note** or
Agentify. It does not read your clipboard.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Billet offers a Shortcuts action and Siri phrase for adding a thought. The text goes straight into a
Billet slot on your Mac. If you use Siri, Apple processes your request under the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Open at Login

If you turn on **Open at Login**, macOS adds Billet to your login items. That setting stays on your
Mac, and you can remove it in **System Settings → General → Login Items**.

## What Billet does not do

- No accounts, sign-ins, or email addresses.
- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No advertising or advertising identifiers.
- No third-party code libraries or SDKs.
- No network connections. Billet runs in Apple's App Sandbox without the outgoing-network
  permission. Links such as this policy open in your web browser, not in Billet.

## Purchases

If you buy Billet through the Mac App Store, Apple processes the payment. The developer receives
only the sales and download reports Apple gives all developers, which do not identify you. If you
have chosen to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may
pass anonymous crash reports for Billet to the developer, who uses them only to fix bugs.

## If you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives whatever you send, including your email address. It is
used only to answer you and fix the problem, and is never sold or shared. Please don't include
private note content unless you're comfortable sharing it. You can ask for your support
correspondence to be deleted at any time.

## Children

Billet is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

## Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal information about Billet users (other than any support
messages you choose to send), there is nothing to access, correct, or export beyond the note files
already under your control. For questions about support correspondence, including requests under
Canada's PIPEDA or other privacy laws, contact the address below.

## Changes

If Billet's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, for example a new feature that needs
the network, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above
will change.
