# Billet

A five-slot thought pad in the macOS menu bar. Write, close, keep. Or turn the thought into a prompt.

Press ⌃⌥Space in any app, type, and close the panel. Each note is a plain Markdown file on your Mac.
**Compact** summarises a note's decisions and actions, and **Agentify** turns it into a standalone
prompt for a coding agent. Both run on Apple's on-device model. Billet then checks every line the
model writes against your note, and anything it can't trace back to your words, such as an invented
name or date, a dropped "don't", or a maybe promoted to a decision, is left out or replaced by your
own sentence.

Billet was built by an analytical chemist who wanted an AI tool that refuses to invent things.

- Requires macOS 26 or later. Compact and Agentify need Apple Intelligence.
- No account, no network access, no analytics.
- Coming to the Mac App Store.
