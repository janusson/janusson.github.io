# Billet Support

Need help? Email [eric@ericjanusson.ca](mailto:eric@ericjanusson.ca). Please include your Billet
version (from **About Billet** in the Storage menu) and your macOS version. You'll usually hear back
within two business days.

## Requirements

- macOS 26 or later.
- Compact and Agentify need a Mac with Apple Intelligence turned on (**System Settings → Apple
  Intelligence & Siri**). Everything else works without it.

## Getting started

- Billet lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. The first time you open it, its panel appears
  under the note icon. After that, click the icon or press **⌃⌥Space** from anywhere.
- You have five slots. Type, close the panel, and your note is saved automatically.
- **Archive** saves the note to the archive and empties the slot.
- **Compact** summarises a note. **Agentify** turns it into a standalone prompt and copies it to your
  clipboard. The note stays in its slot.
- Shortcuts and Siri can add a thought without opening the panel.
- To start Billet when you log in, choose **Storage → Open at Login**.

## Common questions

**Where are my notes?**
Plain Markdown files on your Mac. Choose **Storage → Open Notes Folder** to see them. Slots are in
`inbox/`, archived notes in `archive/`, prompt templates in `Prompts/`.

**How do I back up my notes?**
Copy the notes folder anywhere, or rely on Time Machine. To restore, use **Storage → Import Old
Notes…** and pick the copy. Import fills empty slots and copies the archive; it never changes the
folder you import from.

**Can I sync between Macs?**
Not in the Mac App Store edition. Your notes are ordinary files, so you can copy the notes folder to
another Mac and import it there.

**A file says it conflicted. Did I lose anything?**
No. When two versions of a note disagree, Billet keeps both and puts the extra copy in `Recovered/`
inside the notes folder.

**Compact or Agentify is greyed out or fails.**
Check that Apple Intelligence is on and has finished downloading its model. If the model can't
produce a clear result, Billet tells you and leaves your note and clipboard unchanged. Very long notes
may exceed the model's limit; split them and try again.

**⌃⌥Space doesn't open Billet.**
Another app may already use that shortcut. The panel says so when this happens. Quit or reconfigure
the other app, then relaunch Billet.

**Does Billet send my notes anywhere?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](/billet/privacy/).

**How do I uninstall Billet?**
Quit Billet from the Storage menu, turn off **Open at Login** if you used it, then move Billet to the
Trash. If you also want to remove your notes, open the notes folder first and delete it, or keep it
as a backup.
