## Chemical Analytics from the Bench to the Terminal

Based in British Columbia, Canada, I combine over a decade of experience in chemical analytics, mass spectrometry, and scientific computing to develop robust methods and software for regulated laboratory environments.

## Professional & Academic Experience

My career spans both academic research and industrial applications, focusing on the development of new analytical methods, software tools, and open-source projects that enhance reproducibility and efficiency in chemical analysis.

- **Independent Software Developer (2026 – Present):** Building and maintaining open-source scientific software, including [MassFlow](https://github.com/janusson/MassFlow), a vendor-agnostic MS/MS annotation platform, and [MSMCP](https://github.com/janusson/MSMCP), a model context protocol server for AI integration in mass spectrometry workflows.
- **Research Associate / Acting Technical Lead | TMC Manufacturing (2024 – 2026):** Managed analytical activities supporting industrial-scale ultra-high-purity stable isotope production. Engineered and validated trace-element methods for ≥99.999% (5N+) purity certification and developed assays to improve production processes.
- **Lead Chemist | Delic Labs (2021 – 2022):** Commissioned a federally authorized analytical facility, authored ISO/IEC 17025-aligned SOPs, and developed computational workflows for untargeted mass-spectrometric (DIA-LC-MSMS) profiling that annotated more than 9,500 spectra in botanical products.
- **Postdoctoral Research Associate | University of Glasgow (2017 – 2021):** Developed automated synthetic-analytical platform using high-dimensional LC-TWIMS-HRMS datasets to resolve the real-time self-assembly mechanisms of polyoxometalate (POM) nanomaterials.
- **PhD Researcher | University of Victoria (2012 – 2017):** Investigated the fundamentals of electrospray ionization (ESI) and developed real-time ESI-MS/MS methodologies for monitoring transient catalytic organometallic intermediates.

## Core Competencies

- **Software Development:** Python, pandas, Polars, matchms, pyteomics, spectral-similarity algorithms, automated databasing, interactive visualization, testing, and configuration-driven workflows.
- **Mass Spectrometry & Chemical Separations:** ESI, EI, LC-MS/MS, GC-MS, IC, TWIMS; Orbitrap, QTOF, QqQ, ICP-MS, and magnetic-sector analyzers.
- **Analytical & Regulated R&D:** Method development and validation, ISO/IEC 17025 and cGMP-aligned laboratory operations, 5N+ purity certification, capital procurement, Health Canada Section 56 exemptions, and chain-of-custody controls.

## Open-Source Projects and Publications

- **[MSMCP](https://github.com/janusson/msmcp):** A model context protocol server for AI integration in mass spectrometry workflows, enabling real-time data analysis and decision-making.
- **[PySharpe](https://github.com/janusson/pysharpe):** A modular library for quantitative finance, focusing on portfolio optimization, backtesting, and uncertainty analysis.
